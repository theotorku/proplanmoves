# Product Scope

## In scope for v0.1

### Move categories

- Local residential
- Apartment
- Office
- Labor-only
- Packing service

### User surfaces

- Public marketing and quote-request pages
- Protected admin workspace
- Printable customer quote

### Core domains

- Authentication and roles
- Customers
- Leads and activities
- Pricing configuration
- Estimates
- Quotes
- Jobs
- Dashboard KPIs
- Audit events

## Deferred

- Phone/SMS integration
- AI receptionist
- Photo/video inventory extraction
- Electronic signatures
- Payment collection
- Automated email delivery
- Crew mobile portal
- Truck maintenance
- Inventory/material tracking
- Claims
- Referral program
- Review automation
- Accounting integration
- Advanced calendar and route optimization
- Multi-location or franchise support
- Multi-tenant commercialization

## Scope-control rule

A new feature enters v0.1 only when it is required to complete or secure the critical lead-to-job workflow. All other requests go to `ROADMAP.md`.
