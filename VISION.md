# ProPlan Moves Vision

## Mission

Build the world's first AI-native moving company whose operating system becomes the foundation for a commercial platform serving the entire moving industry.

ProPlan Moves is not simply a moving company.

It is the production environment for ProPlan OS.

Every workflow built here must eventually be reusable by thousands of moving companies.

---

# Core Philosophy

Real business first.

Software second.

AI third.

Revenue validates software.

Software validates AI.

Never reverse that order.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Every feature must solve a real operational problem.

If we wouldn't use it every day ourselves, we shouldn't build it.

---

## Deterministic business logic comes first.

Business rules determine:

- pricing
- scheduling
- compliance
- invoicing
- customer records

AI enhances these workflows.

AI never replaces critical business rules.

---

## Humans remain accountable.

AI can recommend.

Humans approve.

Examples:

✓ Suggest pricing

✓ Draft customer responses

✓ Recommend crew assignments

✗ Final pricing

✗ Refund approval

✗ Legal acceptance

✗ Schedule changes without authorization
